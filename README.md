# Awesome-Field-Service-Management

## Top Field Service Management Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Dispatch, Scheduling, Mobile Workforce, Work Orders, Invoicing & Home/Commercial Service Operations*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Field Service Management (FSM)**. These systems help service businesses schedule jobs, dispatch technicians, manage work orders, track parts, collect payments, and run mobile field operations for trades such as HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and facilities.



**Examples** include ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro, FieldEdge, Service Fusion, mHelpDesk, Zuper, Workiz, Skedulo, and Salesforce Field Service (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Polished commercial FSM platforms dominate the trades market. The strongest open options are **Odoo Community + OCA Field Service**, **ERPNext/Frappe** extensions (including SwiftService), and related CMMS/maintenance tools. This section lists the best available open resources and is realistic about the commercial gap for mobile polish and industry-specific features.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[ServiceTitan](https://www.servicetitan.com/)**  

  Enterprise field service platform built for larger home-service and commercial operations (typically 20+ technicians) with advanced dispatch, price books, marketing, and reporting.



- **[Jobber](https://www.getjobber.com/)**  

  Popular FSM platform for solo to mid-size crews—scheduling, quoting, invoicing, client history, and mobile apps with transparent pricing and fast onboarding.



- **[Housecall Pro](https://www.housecallpro.com/)**  

  All-rounder field service software for small-to-mid teams with strong mobile apps, online booking, payments, and built-in marketing tools.



- **[FieldEdge](https://www.fieldedge.com/)**  

  Field service management solution focused on HVAC, plumbing, and electrical contractors with dispatch, inventory, and mobile capabilities.



- **[Service Fusion](https://www.servicefusion.com/)**  

  All-in-one FSM platform for service businesses covering scheduling, dispatch, invoicing, and customer management.



- **[mHelpDesk](https://www.mhelpdesk.com/)**  

  Field service and work-order management software for contractors and service companies.



- **[Zuper](https://www.zuper.co/)**  

  Modern field service platform with scheduling, mobile workforce tools, and customer experience features.



- **[Workiz](https://www.workiz.com/)**  

  Field service software popular with smaller teams for job scheduling, dispatch, invoicing, and payments.



- **[Skedulo](https://www.skedulo.com/)**  

  Intelligent workforce management and scheduling platform for complex field service and mobile workforce operations.



- **[Salesforce Field Service](https://www.salesforce.com/products/field-service/overview/)**  

  Enterprise field service solution tightly integrated with Salesforce CRM for large organizations needing advanced scheduling and service operations.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[OCA Field Service (Odoo)](https://github.com/OCA/field-service)**  

  Leading open-source field service modules for Odoo Community—covers field-service orders, routes, recurring work, agreements, skills, vehicles, stock, and integration with CRM/inventory/accounting.



- **[ERPNext / Frappe](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  

  Open-source ERP with maintenance, support, inventory, and project modules that can be configured for field service and work-order workflows.



- **[SwiftService](https://github.com/vinaymishraofficial/swiftservice)**  

  Open-source field service app for Frappe/ERPNext covering service requests, engineer dispatch, GPS check-in/out, spare parts, repair, billing, and closure.



- **[openMAINT](https://www.openmaint.org/)**  

  Open-source CMMS and facility maintenance platform useful for asset-centric and facilities field service operations.



- **[LibreWO and small work-order open tools](https://github.com/)**  

  Lightweight open work-order systems suitable for small repair shops and simple service operations.



- **[OpenBoxes and inventory open tools](https://github.com/openboxes/openboxes)**  

  Open inventory and supply-chain components that can support parts management in field service contexts.



- **[Mobile GPS and check-in open libraries](https://github.com/)**  

  Community tools for location tracking, geofencing, and technician check-in that can complement open FSM stacks.



- **[Scheduling and dispatch open optimizers](https://github.com/)**  

  Open routing and scheduling libraries usable for basic technician assignment and route planning prototypes.



- **[Invoicing and payment open integrations](https://github.com/)**  

  Stripe and other payment connectors commonly paired with open ERP/FSM systems for field billing.



- **[Documentation and implementation open guides](https://github.com/)**  

  Community resources for deploying and customizing Odoo/ERPNext field service configurations.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Starting with **Odoo Community + OCA Field Service** when you need real FSM workflows (orders, routes, recurring service, inventory links) on an open stack.

- Using **ERPNext + SwiftService** when field service must live inside a broader open ERP (CRM, inventory, accounting, purchasing).

- Accepting that highly polished mobile apps, industry-specific price books, same-day payments, marketing automation, and large-scale enterprise dispatch still favor commercial platforms (ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro, Salesforce Field Service, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on data ownership, lower long-term cost, and tight integration with open ERP systems.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Run Odoo or ERPNext as the system of record → enable OCA Field Service or SwiftService modules → equip technicians with mobile-friendly views and GPS check-in → manage parts and invoices in the same ERP → optionally layer commercial tools for advanced dispatch or payments. Suitable for organizations with implementation capacity. Most home-service and mid-market contractors rely on commercial FSM platforms for speed of deployment and mobile experience.
